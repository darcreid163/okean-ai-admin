# Billing and Usage Ledger
Cloud Core/Gateway ledger is the only source of truth; Native and telemetry never calculate or mutate balance.
Approved tariff (final owner decision 16.11.2026; canonical in `wp-ai-admin-cloud/config/plans.json`, policy `tariffs-v5-20261116`): Free 0 ₽ — 50 actions/UTC day; Daily 800 ₽ — 500; Pro 2,000 ₽ — 2,000; PRO+ (`business`) 5,000 ₽ — 5,000; PRO++ (`businesspro`) 10,000 ₽ — 10,000. Every tariff is counted per UTC day; all five quotas are approved (`prices_approved: true`). `business`/`businesspro` are stable internal slugs; PRO+/PRO++ are display names only. `schemes.tariffs_v3` is the only active scheme and `default_scheme`; `legacy_monthly_v1`, `tariffs_v2`, `legacy_plans` and the `power`/`agency`/`scale` slugs are retired and not assignable. Historical accounts and licences resolve to the equivalent final daily plan at read time and are rewritten by the idempotent, audited `Core::migrateRetiredSchemes()` cutover (unknown slugs skipped and audited, historical monthly balances preserved). All tools available on Free. Batch is enabled as `successful_object_v1` (per successful object); an explicitly stored `wpai_gateway_batch_object_v1` option is the operator kill switch. Verify live config and QuotaPolicy before policy changes. No automatic billing or live YooKassa without separate approval.
Charge successful billable operations server-side, including successful reads. Batch = one action per successful object; failed/skipped objects free. Validation, permission denial, rejected and failed requests free. Use atomic reservation/finalization and stable idempotency keys; retries/timeouts/lost responses cannot double charge. Handle partial success, quota exhaustion mid-batch, rollback, restart and uncertain outcomes. Ledger records account/site, operation, idempotency reference, object outcomes, policy version, timestamp, charge and signed receipt. Never trust client counters or lossy telemetry. No negative balance or untracked successful mutation. Response returns charged, success/failure counts, limit, used/remaining, period/reset and receipt. Test 0/1/limit-1/limit/over, concurrency, replay, partial batch, rollback, timeout, DB failure and dashboard refresh reconciliation.
