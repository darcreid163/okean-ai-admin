# Billing and Usage Ledger
Cloud Core/Gateway ledger is the only source of truth; Native and telemetry never calculate or mutate balance.
Approved tariff (owner decision 06.11.2026; canonical in `wp-ai-admin-cloud/config/plans.json` as `tariffs-v3-20261106`): free 100/day, daily 500/day, pro 2,000/day, business 5,000/day — every plan a UTC calendar-day quota period; there is no BusinessPro tier. Legacy monthly (`legacy_monthly_v1`) accounts keep their monthly terms until an explicit audited transfer. All tools available on Free. Verify live config and QuotaPolicy before policy changes. No automatic billing or live YooKassa without separate approval.
Charge successful billable operations server-side, including successful reads. Batch = one action per successful object; failed/skipped objects free. Validation, permission denial, rejected and failed requests free. Use atomic reservation/finalization and stable idempotency keys; retries/timeouts/lost responses cannot double charge. Handle partial success, quota exhaustion mid-batch, rollback, restart and uncertain outcomes. Ledger records account/site, operation, idempotency reference, object outcomes, policy version, timestamp, charge and signed receipt. Never trust client counters or lossy telemetry. No negative balance or untracked successful mutation. Response returns charged, success/failure counts, limit, used/remaining, period/reset and receipt. Test 0/1/limit-1/limit/over, concurrency, replay, partial batch, rollback, timeout, DB failure and dashboard refresh reconciliation.
